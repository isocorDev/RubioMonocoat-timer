How much to mix

Rubio rates Oil Plus 2C at 300 to 500 sq ft per liter, and open grain like red oak sits at the low end. STEP areas count every face and edge of a part, so the estimate runs a little high for parts that end up hidden in joinery. Mixed oil can't be saved, so if your test board used less, raise the coverage number.

Settings and alerts

Where the numbers come from: Rubio's data sheet says let the oil absorb a few minutes, remove all excess within 30 minutes at most, and use mixed oil within 4 to 6 hours. Rubio's 2024 US floor instructions say let it react at least 5 minutes, start removing excess around 15 minutes, and finish by 30. A space heater makes the air warm and dry, which shortens the window, so if the oil feels tacky when you wipe, lower the sit time. Bleed-back checks every 30 minutes for 4 hours are shop practice for open-grain wood, not a Rubio spec.

Hands-free control. The big Next button always does the most urgent thing. A Bluetooth keyboard or a page-turner foot pedal works too: Space, Enter, Page Down or Right arrow for Next, and Page Up, Left arrow or Backspace for Undo. Voice control listens for "next", "wiped", "checked", "apply" and "undo" in Chrome, Edge and Safari when the page is opened from its own web address.
