Rubio Monocoat Oil Plus 2CFinish timers

How much to mix

The estimate uses your sit time and wet-surface limit from Settings: you apply a group, wait for the first one to reach its sit time, then wipe the group. Rubio rates Oil Plus 2C at 300 to 500 sq ft per liter, with open grain like red oak at the low end, so 300 is a safe default. Mixed oil can't be saved, so if your test board used less, lower the coverage number.

Before you leave the shop

Settings and alerts

Where the numbers come from: Rubio's data sheet says let the oil absorb a few minutes and remove all excess within 30 minutes at most, and use mixed oil within 4 to 6 hours. Rubio's 2024 US floor instructions say let it react at least 5 minutes, start removing excess around 15 minutes, and finish by 30. A 15 minute sit sits at the long end of that range. A space heater makes the air warm and dry, which shortens the window, so if the oil feels tacky or drags when you wipe, drop the sit time to 10.

Bleed-back checks are shop practice for open-grain wood, not a Rubio spec: check every 30 minutes for about 4 hours and wipe any beads. Keep this page open with the screen on; phones pause timers in background tabs, though the times stay accurate when you come back.
